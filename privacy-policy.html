FootTopBusiness is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information with formal responsibility. This policy details our data management practices, processing procedures, and user rights within the platform.

Executive Summary We only process data required to operate the application, facilitate stadium reservations, deliver system communications, and maintain infrastructure security.

Information Collected

Account Identifiers

  • Full legal name
  • Phone contact number
  • System username
  • Assigned administrative role (Owner, Coach, Admin)
  • Optional profile imagery

Geographic Location

With explicit authorization, the application accesses location services exclusively while active. This enables coordinate resolution for accurate venue identification on interactive maps.

Note: Background location collection is strictly non-operational.

Business Operations

  • Facility profiles (designation, address, rate structures, operational hours)
  • Reservation registers and historical logs
  • Customer contact records linked to bookings
  • Financial yields and tournament analytical data

Technical Telemetry

  • Messaging endpoint tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Network connectivity status for synchronizing state

Purpose of Processing

  • Execution of booking schedules, matches, and tournament fixtures
  • Provision of role-restricted access to administrative tools
  • Dispatch of critical system notifications
  • Identity authentication and access threat prevention

Third-Party Integrations

Personal data is not commercialized or sold. Infrastructure services are limited to trusted technical providers required for operational viability:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Push notifications and app telemetry
  • OpenStreetMap / OpenFreeMap: Mapping interfaces and geocoding
  • Private Infrastructure: Encrypted backend databases

System Permissions

Identifier Functional Purpose
INTERNET Maintains active link to application servers for data synchronization.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Renders user position to assist coordinate selection for venues.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers real-time status updates regarding reservations and schedules.

Retention & Erasure

Data is maintained only for periods required to fulfill operational services or for the duration of active accounts.

Account deletion requests may be initiated via a District or Super Administrator. Upon confirmation, associated personal records are purged from active primary databases.

Security Protocol

Information security is enforced through standardized technical controls, including JSON Web Token (JWT) authorization protocols and secure local storage mechanisms for active sessions.

Amendments

Modifications to this document will be published directly within the platform. Revisions take effect upon publication with updated timestamp references.
