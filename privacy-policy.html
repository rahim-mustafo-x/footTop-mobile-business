Privacy Policy

FootTopBusiness • Last updated: October 27, 2026

FootTopBusiness is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information responsibly. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when using our application.

In short: We collect only the information necessary to operate the app, manage stadium bookings, send notifications, and keep your account secure.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Username
  • Account role (Owner, Coach, Admin, etc.)
  • Optional profile image

Location Information

With your permission, the app may access your location while you are using the map. This allows us to display your current position and help you accurately select stadium coordinates.

We do not collect location data in the background.

Business & Booking Data

  • Stadium details (name, address, pricing, working hours)
  • Booking and reservation records
  • Customer contact information provided during bookings
  • Revenue and tournament statistics

Technical Information

  • Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Network connectivity status for synchronization

How We Use Your Information

  • Manage bookings, tournaments, and schedules
  • Provide role-based access to business features
  • Send real-time notifications and updates
  • Authenticate users and prevent unauthorized access

Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information. We use trusted third-party services that help the application function properly.

  • Firebase (Google): Notifications and analytics
  • OpenStreetMap / OpenFreeMap: Maps and address lookup
  • Private Backend Servers: Secure storage of application data

App Permissions

Permission Purpose
INTERNET Connect to our servers and synchronize data
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Display your location and select stadium coordinates
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notify you about bookings, matches, and updates

Data Retention

Your information is stored only as long as necessary to provide our services or while your account remains active.

You may request deletion of your account through a District or Super Admin. Once approved, your personal information will be removed from our active systems.

Security

We protect your data using industry-standard security practices, including JWT-based authentication and secure on-device storage for sensitive credentials.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published within the application or on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised.
