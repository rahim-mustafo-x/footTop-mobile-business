# Privacy Policy for FootTopBusiness

**Last Updated: October 27, 2023**

Welcome to **FootTopBusiness**. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our service to you:

### A. Personal Information
- **Account Data**: When you are registered by an administrator, we store your full name, phone number, username, and role (e.g., Owner, Coach, Admin).
- **Profile Image**: You may optionally provide a profile image URL to personalize your account.

### B. Location Data
- **Precise Location**: With your permission, we use `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` and `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` to:
    - Show your current position on the map.
    - Help you precisely select the geographic coordinates for your stadium.
- **Background Location**: We **do not** collect your location in the background. Location is only accessed while you are using the Map view.

### C. Booking and Business Data
- **Stadium Information**: Name, address, hourly rates, and working hours of the stadiums you manage.
- **Booking Records**: Details of reservations made through the system, including customer names and phone numbers (if provided).
- **Revenue Stats**: Financial summaries of match bookings and tournament entries.

### D. Device and Technical Data
- **Push Notification Tokens**: We collect device tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) to send you real-time alerts about bookings, matches, and system updates.
- **Network State**: We check your internet connection status to ensure data synchronization with our servers.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- **Service Provision**: To manage stadium bookings, tournaments, and schedules.
- **Role-Based Access**: To ensure that only authorized personnel (Admins/Owners) can access financial reports and management tools.
- **Communication**: To send push notifications regarding your business activity.
- **Security**: To verify your identity during login and prevent unauthorized access.

---

## 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do **not** sell your personal data. However, we may share information with third-party services necessary for app functionality:
- **Firebase (Google)**: For sending push notifications and analytics.
- **OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap**: Used for rendering map tiles and reverse geocoding addresses. No personal identity is shared with these tile providers.
- **Internal Backend**: Your data is securely stored on our private servers (`83.222.19.225`).

---

## 4. Permissions Explained

| Permission | Reason |
| :--- | :--- |
| `INTERNET` | Required to communicate with our servers. |
| `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` | Required to show "You are here" on the map. |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Required to alert you about new bookings or matches. |

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

- **Retention**: We keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with services.
- **Deletion**: You can request account deletion by contacting your District or Super Admin. Once deleted, your personal data will be removed from our active databases.

---

## 6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including:
- **JWT Authentication**: All communication between the app and the server is secured using JSON Web Tokens.
- **Encrypted Storage**: Sensitive data like your access token is stored securely on your device using Android/iOS internal preferences.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app or on this page.
